## The Raman gain spectrum of standard single-mode fibre (GNPy, BSD-3-Clause)

`crates/com-channel/src/optical/raman/gain.rs` (`RamanGain::ssmf`, the tables
`SSMF_OFFSETS_THZ` and `SSMF_GAIN_M_W`) transcribes, digit for digit, the default Raman
coefficient of GNPy (`gnpy.core.parameters.DEFAULT_RAMAN_COEFFICIENT`): the gain
efficiency $g_R A_{\mathrm{eff}}$ of standard single-mode fibre at 90 frequency offsets,
for a pump at 206.18 THz. GNPy is the optical network planning library of the Telecom
Infra Project, <https://github.com/Telecominfraproject/oopt-gnpy>, released under the
BSD 3-Clause licence reproduced below.

## The recorded test oracles (`tests/oracle/`)

The fixtures of `tests/oracle/` are **outputs** of OptiCommPy (GPL-3.0,
<https://github.com/edsonportosilva/OptiCommPy>) and of GNPy (BSD-3-Clause), computed
on inputs generated by the tests and recorded with the version of the tool: numbers,
not code. They contain no source code nor data file of either package, and neither
package is imported by com-rs or by its tests outside the recording step
(`COMRS_RECORD_ORACLE=1`, see `tests/oracle.py`).

## The recorded AFF3CT outputs (`validation/aff3ct/`)

The fixtures `validation/aff3ct/*.json` are **outputs** of AFF3CT, the forward error
correction toolbox of A. Cassagne et al. (*SoftwareX* 10, 100345, 2019,
<https://github.com/aff3ct/aff3ct>, MIT licence, Copyright (c) 2017-2026 aff3ct): the
frame, bit and frame-error counts of its simulations, with the command line and the
version that produced them (`tools/record_aff3ct.py`). They contain no source code nor
data file of AFF3CT, and AFF3CT is neither linked nor called by com-rs or by its tests
outside the recording step. The inputs AFF3CT read during the recording are written by
com-rs: the LDPC parity-check matrices of `validation/aff3ct/matrices/` (progressive
edge growth constructions of `com_fec::ldpc`, in the `alist` format) and the polar
frozen sets of `validation/aff3ct/frozen/` (`com_fec::polar`). The matrices that come
with AFF3CT (its `conf` submodule, `github.com/aff3ct/configuration_files`, which carries
no licence file) are not redistributed. The reference database of AFF3CT
(`github.com/aff3ct/error_rate_references`, no licence) was read for its command lines
only: none of its files nor numbers are copied.
